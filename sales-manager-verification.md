# Sales Manager Verification

The protected Libaas by HAYA Studio loaded the new Sales Manager after administrator authentication. It displayed both live catalog products, including the Azure Garden Three-Piece Suit and Sandstone Column Abaya, and provided the regular price, sale price, percentage-discount, Save Sale, and Delete Product controls.

The controls are protected by the administrator token. Product deletion uses a confirmation dialog and removes the product from the public Libaas storefront while retaining historical order records.
